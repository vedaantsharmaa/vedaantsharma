    B.Tech Final Year Project 🏆 Best Project of the Year — 2024

    IoT-Based Smart Irrigation Monitoring System

    An IoT-enabled smart irrigation system designed to improve water efficiency, nutrient delivery, and crop-growing conditions through real-time monitoring and automated control.

    The Problem

    Traditional agriculture often faces pressure to produce more food using limited resources. Manual irrigation and fertilisation can be imprecise, leading to water wastage, over-fertilisation, uneven crop growth, and reduced resource efficiency.

    The Solution

    The system uses real-time soil-moisture data to automate irrigation, fertilisation, cooling, and sunlight exposure. It helps support precise resource usage while promoting better crop productivity and sustainable agricultural practices.

    My Contribution

    I contributed to the design and development of the IoT-based monitoring and automation system, integrating sensor-based inputs with relay-controlled irrigation, fertiliser, cooling, and lighting mechanisms.

    Technology Used

    • ESP32 WROOM Dev Kit
    • REES52 soil moisture sensor
    • TDS sensor
    • DHT11 humidity sensor
    • LDR module
    • Piezo mist module
    • 4-channel relay module
    • Water and fertiliser motors

    Recognition & Publication

    The project was published in IEEE and received the Best Project of the Year Award in 2024.
